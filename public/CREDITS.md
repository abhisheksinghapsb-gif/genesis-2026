# GENESIS 2026 — Asset & Third-Party Credits

This project honors open-source licensing, creative commons attributions, and design credits.

## Visual Design Reference & Style
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Inspired by *Brawl Stars* (Supercell) UI art direction (chunky typography, drop-base buttons, beveled angled plaques, and game HUD elements). All UI components are re-engineered natively from scratch using SVG and CSS.
- **Event Branding:** GENESIS 2026 (5th Edition) — GDXR (AR/VR) Club, Army Institute of Technology (AIT), Pune.

## Fonts
- **Lilita One:** Designed by Juan Montoreano. Licensed under the [SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1](http://scripts.sil.org/OFL). Self-hosted via Next.js Font Optimization.
- **Nunito:** Designed by Vernon Adams, Cyreal. Licensed under the [SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1](http://scripts.sil.org/OFL).

## 3D Graphics & Shaders
- **Procedural Sky & Celestial Bodies:** Low-poly moon, ringed Saturn-like planet, and twinkling starfields are computed dynamically at runtime using procedural geometries and custom GLSL/Three.js shaders. (Zero downloaded asset weight).
- **3D Assets & Models:** Any optional 3D models strictly follow CC0 or CC-BY terms (Poly Pizza / Kenney), Draco compressed to < 300 KB.

## Mockup Designs
- Provided flat 1440x1024 mockup source sheets in `design-mockups/` are authored by the GDXR Design Team. Transparent layers extracted for animated interactive scenes with permission.
